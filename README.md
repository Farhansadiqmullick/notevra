# Notevra

**Offline first, Sync Later.**

Notevra is a notes, tasks and planning app that saves every change on your device, keeps working
with no signal, and syncs when you're back online. Use it on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows.
It's free.

[Website](https://notevra.farhanmullick.trade/) ·
[Open the app](https://blocnote.farhanmullick.trade) ·

## Why Notevra

- **Works offline, for real.** Every change is written to your device first, so the app never waits
  on the network. No signal on the train? Keep writing. It syncs once you're back online.
- **No account needed.** Notevra works fully without one. Sign in only if you want your notes on
  more than one device, or want to share them with other people.
- **You decide on a clash.** If the same thing changed on your phone and your laptop, Notevra shows
  only what differs and lets you choose: keep mine, keep theirs, or keep both.
- **Your notes stay yours.** Export everything, images included, as one file and bring it back
  anytime.

## Features

### Write in blocks

- Every line is a block you can move, convert or nest. Type **/** anywhere to pick from 15 block
  types: tasks, headings, lists, toggles, callouts, quotes, code, tables, images, videos, bookmarks,
  long text and dividers.
- Drag blocks to reorder them, or select several and copy, duplicate, delete or move them together.
- Nest tasks up to three levels deep with **Tab**; **Shift+Tab** brings one back out.
- Give any block its own text or background colour, and its own text size.
- Undo and redo on every page.

### Formatting that shows as you type

- Write `**bold**` and it turns bold straight away. Italic, strikethrough, inline code, links,
  superscript and subscript all work the same way, or from the format bar.
- Start a line with `- `, `1. `, `[] `, `> `, `# ` or `---` to turn it into a list, task, quote,
  heading or divider.
- Paste from Slack, GitHub or ChatGPT and the lists, tasks, headings and code blocks come through
  intact.
- Works in Bengali too.

### Three kinds of page

Pick the shape that fits the job when you create a page:

| Page | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| **Blank** | A meeting note, a decision, a journal entry. One focused page. |
| **Planning** | A long plan with an outline on the side and a progress bar that fills as tasks get done. |
| **Archive** | A backlog or a shopping list: a task list on the left, the selected item's details on the right. |

Every page can carry a status, owner, assignee, due date, priority, tags and linked pages.

### Stay on top of your day

- **Today** shows everything due or snoozed to today, across every page.
- **Inbox** catches quick tasks before you file them.
- **Snooze** a task and it comes back later with a tone and a notice.
- **Pin** important pages and tasks to the top.
- Due-date reminders at a time you choose.

### Find anything

Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K**) to search every page, jump to one, or insert a block. Search runs on
your device, so it works offline too.

### Images and video

- Paste screenshots and photos straight into a page. They shrink on your device to save space.
- Paste a YouTube, Vimeo or Dailymotion link to embed it. Videos load only when you tap them.

### Share with people

- Put pages in a **project** and invite people by email or by their display name.
- Choose what each person can do: **Admin**, **Member** or **Guest** (read-only).
- Nobody joins until they accept, and anyone can leave at any time.
- A notification bell tells you about invites and replies, and the activity feed shows who changed
  what.

### Never lose a version

- **Version history** keeps earlier versions of a page for 30 days. Restore any of them.
- Deleted pages and projects go to **Trash** first, with Undo.
- Archived pages stay out of the way and are never removed on their own.

### Make it yours

- Six colour themes, each in light and dark: Indigo, Violet, Ocean, Sky, Grape and Graphite.
- Four fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Lora and JetBrains Mono.
- Tag colours that suit both themes, or pick your own. Rename and merge tags in Settings.

### Bring your notes with you

- Import your **Google Keep** notes from a Google Takeout file. Checklists become tasks, labels
  become tags, and images come along.
- Export everything from Notevra as one file and import it again on any device.

### Sign in your way

- Email and password, or **Continue with Google**.
- Updates wait for you: a new version never reloads the app while you're typing.

## Install

**iPhone, iPad and Android** install straight from the browser: no app store needed. Open
[Notevra](https://blocnote.farhanmullick.trade) and add it to your home screen. It gets its own icon,
opens full screen and works offline.

- **iPhone & iPad:** open in Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android:** open in Chrome → *Install app*.

**Mac and Windows** have a desktop app that updates itself with one click.
[Download the latest version](https://github.com/Farhansadiqmullick/notevra-releases/releases/latest).

> Notevra isn't signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so the first launch needs one
> extra step. The [website](https://notevra.farhanmullick.trade/#install) walks you through it.

## Questions

**Where are my notes stored?**
On your device first. If you sign in, they also sync to your account so your other devices get them.

**What happens if I'm offline?**
Everything keeps working. Changes wait on your device and sync once you're back online.

**Why isn't it in the App Store or Play Store?**
Notevra installs from the browser as a web app. You get the home-screen icon, full-screen window and
offline use without a store download, and updates arrive without waiting for a review.

**Can I take my notes out?**
Yes. Settings → Sync & Storage exports everything, images included, as one file.
